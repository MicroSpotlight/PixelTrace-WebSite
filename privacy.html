隐私政策

适用版本 PixelTrace 0.1.0 · 上架前草案

照片与拍摄信息

你可以通过 iOS 选择授予部分或完整的相册访问权限。PixelTrace 使用授权照片的拍摄时间、GPS、相机信息和系统相册来源，在设备上生成影像轨迹、搜索和筛选照片，并展示拍摄信息。扫描范围、语言和外观偏好也保存在设备上。

地图和 iCloud 照片

轨迹地图与海报地图由 Apple 地图提供。扫描已授权照片和查看轨迹详情时，PixelTrace 会将需要查询地址的拍摄点坐标交由 Apple 地图反查，返回的地址在设备上临时缓存，有效期为 7 天。使用时间线的地点搜索时，你输入的地名或地址交由 Apple 地图查询，照片的 GPS 信息在设备上和查询结果匹配。预览所选照片时，iOS 照片服务可能从 iCloud 加载该照片。Apple 服务的数据处理由 Apple 的隐私政策说明。

分享

你选择分享照片或海报时，系统分享面板会将所选文件交给你指定的接收方。海报可包含拍摄日期、地点和相机型号。

管理访问与本机数据

你可在 iOS 设置中更改或撤销 PixelTrace 的相册权限。删除 App 会移除该设备上的应用数据。你已分享或导出的文件由相应的接收方或保存位置管理。

开发者信息

PixelTrace 由 MicroSpotlight 开发。开发者信息可在 MicroSpotlight 组织主页 查看。

Privacy policy

PixelTrace 0.1.0 · Pre-release draft

Photos and capture information

You choose whether to grant limited or full Photos access in iOS. PixelTrace uses capture dates, GPS, camera information and Photos source categories from authorized photos to build journeys, search and filter photos, and show photo details on your device. Scan range, language and appearance preferences are stored on the device.

Maps and iCloud photos

Apple Maps supplies maps for journeys and posters. When scanning authorized photos or displaying journey details, PixelTrace sends capture coordinates to Apple Maps as needed to find addresses. The results are cached on your device for seven days. When you search for a place in the timeline, Apple Maps looks up the place name or address you enter. PixelTrace matches photo GPS data against the results on your device. When you view a photo, the iOS Photos service may retrieve it from iCloud. Apple’s privacy policy describes how Apple handles data for its services.

Sharing

When you share a photo or poster, the system share sheet passes the selected file to the destination you choose. A poster may contain capture dates, locations and camera models.

Manage access and local data

You can change or revoke PixelTrace’s Photos permission in iOS Settings. Deleting the app removes its app data from the device. Previously shared and exported files are managed by the recipients or destinations you selected.

Developer information

PixelTrace is developed by MicroSpotlight. Visit the MicroSpotlight organization page for developer information.
