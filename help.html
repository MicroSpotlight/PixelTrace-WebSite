帮助与支持

如何生成第一条影像轨迹？

在引导页点「导入照片」，然后在系统弹窗中选择部分或完整访问。稍后也可以从「轨迹」页右上角的加号导入。含有 GPS 信息的照片会出现在地图上，其他照片可以在时间线浏览。

如何调整授权或扫描范围？

打开 PixelTrace 的「设置 → 相册权限」。你可以调整扫描范围、管理已授权的照片，或打开 iOS 设置更改相册访问权限。

如何查看拍摄信息或分享海报？

轻点照片进入全屏预览，向上滑动查看拍摄时间、设备和地点。要分享一段旅程，请打开轨迹详情，轻点右上角的分享按钮并选择海报样式。

反馈问题

请在 GitHub 提交问题，附上 PixelTrace 版本、iOS 版本和可以复现问题的步骤。

Help and support

How do I create my first journey?

Tap Import Photos on the welcome screen, then choose limited or full access in the iOS prompt. You can also import later using the plus button in Trails. Photos with GPS appear on the map. Other photos remain available in the timeline.

How do I manage access or the scan range?

In PixelTrace, open Settings and tap Photo Access. From there you can change the scan range, manage selected photos, or open iOS Settings to change Photos permissions.

How do I see photo details or share a poster?

Tap a photo to view it in full screen and swipe up for the capture date, camera and location. To share a journey, open its details, tap the share button, and pick a poster style.

Report a problem

Open a GitHub issue with your PixelTrace and iOS versions and the steps to reproduce the problem.
